FOUR ALERT TYPES REQUESTED BY CECI

Separate recipient list and send button for each
Google sign-in required to send test emails

Use the Google account that has Purchase Executive Dashboard access in the Equestrian Labs user registry. The email service will not be opened publicly.

ALERT 01TEST EMAIL

Gross margin below 20%

Products sold during the previous week whose gross margin finished below 20%. The eventual automated report should identify the affected products and their margin.

ALERT 02TEST EMAIL

Critically low sell-through

Products materially below the expected sell-through at 30, 60 or 90 days from the inventory receipt date. The final rule must use the approved goals and flag only significant gaps.

ALERT 03TEST EMAIL

Stock at risk

Products whose available stock may be insufficient, assessed against the comparable period last year or its three-month average to respect seasonal demand. The comparison method remains to be validated.

ALERT 04TEST EMAIL

Discount exceptions

Orders with a discount greater than 20%, or more than one discount code applied to the same order. The automated report should identify the order and discount details.
